Active Statutory Escalation Rules

Standard Operational Trigger

Rule #ESC-01: Escalate after 2 days overdue with 2 unacknowledged reminders.

Level 1 (Supervisor)

Triggered when any citizen grievance exceeds SAKALA statutory resolution timeline by >48 hours AND 2 automated reminders (SMS/IVR) remain unacknowledged by the nodal officer.

Level 2 (Department Head & DC)

Triggered when an L1 docket remains unattended for an additional 48 hours (>96h total breach) OR when civic health risk score drops below 35. Transmits summons to Principal Secretary.

Grace Period & Exceptions

Emergency water contamination and cold chain vaccine stockouts automatically bypass L1 directly to L2 after 12h.

Authority Safeguard

Statutory Administrative Routing Notice

“Escalation notifies higher officials only. It does not decide on allegations or legal action.”

Supervisory escalation serves as an administrative routing and compliance acceleration mechanism under Section 12 of the Karnataka Guarantee of Services to Citizens Act. Adjudication of misconduct or financial penal deductions is conducted exclusively by the Sakala Appellate Tribunal through formal Section 14 proceedings.
